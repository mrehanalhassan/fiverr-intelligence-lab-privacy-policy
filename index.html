Privacy Policy

Fiverr Intelligence Lab for Chrome · version 1.1.0 · effective 31 October 2026

In short: Fiverr Intelligence Lab keeps your data in your own browser, with automatic backups there and — if you allow it — a private copy of your conversations and settings in your own Google account through Chrome Sync. MR ToolLab runs no server for this extension and never receives, sells or shares your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Data leaves your browser only (1) when the extension reads Fiverr pages for you, (2) when you use the AI chat — then your message and the Fiverr data needed to answer it go to the AI provider you chose, with your API key — and (3) through Chrome Sync, if it is on.

Fiverr Intelligence Lab is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Fiverr International Ltd. "Fiverr" is a trademark of its owner.

2. Chrome Sync

If you are signed in to Chrome with sync on (including Extensions) and keep "Sync with Chrome Sync" on in the extension, your conversations and settings are also stored with Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API so they appear on every Chrome signed in to your Google account and come back on a new computer. This copy lives in your Google account under Google's Privacy Policy; MR ToolLab cannot access it. API keys, saved Fiverr pages, rank history and profile snapshots are not synced. Turn it off in Settings → Data & storage.

3. When data leaves your browser

  • Fiverr. The extension opens public Fiverr pages (search results, gigs, seller profiles) in your browser — in hidden frames or a background tab — exactly as if you visited them, using your normal browser session. It only does this for what you ask for, for Live Mode on pages you browse, and for the scheduled refresh of your own profile. It never reads your Fiverr messages, orders, payments, password or cookies, and never sends anything to Fiverr on your behalf (no messages, no orders, no edits).
  • Robot checks. When Fiverr asks for a "Press & Hold" check, the extension stops all requests and waits for you to complete it yourself. It never solves or bypasses such checks.
  • AI providers (only if you use the AI chat). Your message, the conversation so far and the Fiverr data needed to answer are sent directly from your browser to the provider you connected (for example Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, GitHub Models, NVIDIA, Cerebras, Cohere, Hugging Face, Z.ai, xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, a local model on your own computer, or a custom endpoint you enter), authenticated with your own API key. That provider's privacy policy and terms apply to what you send. MR ToolLab does not see these requests.
  • Chrome Sync as described in section 2.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no remote code — all code ships inside the extension.

5. Your control

  • Keep conversations & rank history for 30 days, 90 days (default), 1 year or always — older ones are deleted automatically (conversations on all synced devices when sync is on).
  • Automatic clean-up of cached Fiverr data, on by default, with a "Clean up now" button. You are asked about Chrome Sync and automatic clean-up the first time you open the extension.
  • View and delete any stored category or single record in Settings → Data & storage; "Delete all data" removes everything except settings and AI keys.
  • Factory reset removes everything, including settings and AI keys (and, with sync on, the synced conversations and settings).
  • Restore any automatic backup, or export a conversation as a file.
  • Turn off Chrome Sync for the extension in Settings → Data & storage.
  • Uninstalling removes the local data from Chrome. The Chrome Sync copy stays in your Google account until you delete it in the extension or clear your Chrome sync data.
  • Optional permissions can be removed at chrome://extensions → Fiverr Intelligence Lab → Details.

6. Children

The extension is a tool for Fiverr sellers and is not directed at children. MR ToolLab does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and does not collect any data at all.
